Method

REAL SERVICES

S3 and DynamoDB in us-east-1, on-demand rates. No LocalStack, no emulator, no simulated latency.

METERED AT THE WIRE

Operation counts come from the SDK; DynamoDB capacity comes from AWS's own ConsumedCapacity field, not from our arithmetic.

CI-GATED

Each anchor is an assertion. A regression fails the build — and so does a figure that gets better than the code deserves.

What these numbers do not establish

Repeated verbatim on every page that quotes a figure, in the same panel as the figure.

01

Published rates × wire-metered ops — not a cloud bill. Request tiers and storage-class transitions are unmeasured; Cost Explorer reconciliation is blocked on the account.

02

Cost, not latency. One scoped latency run is published above; everything else on this page is a bill, not a stopwatch.

03

Your region and workload move the crossover. Feed your own PricingProfile into the shipped estimateCost() — the same function this page is generated from, so the site cannot drift ahead of the library. How →

04

It is pre-1.0 and single-maintainer. The real risk is operational maturity, not losing the bytes — they are immutable, documented, and in your bucket. exportSegments() is a one-command exit.
