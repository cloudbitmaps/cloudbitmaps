# Adopters

Teams and projects that use CloudBitmaps. If you use it, in production, in staging or while you evaluate it, we would
like to know: it is how the project learns what to build next and when the format is ready to freeze at `1.0`.

Tell us in whichever way suits you:

- **Add a row below** in a pull request.
- **Open a ["We use CloudBitmaps" issue](https://github.com/cloudbitmaps/cloudbitmaps/issues/new?template=adopter.yml).**
  It asks whether you want to be listed here. Leave the box unticked and you are counted (every such issue carries the
  `adopter` label, and the count is of those issues) but not added to this file.
- **Post in [Discussions › Show and tell](https://github.com/cloudbitmaps/cloudbitmaps/discussions/categories/show-and-tell)**
  how you use it and what got in your way.
