An encrypted generation's cold open sends its tail read beside the key request instead of after it, for the opens that start from the object (`iterate`, a combine, a pin), so their wall time is the longer of the two and not the sum. The footer is still held to the row's summary and the index decrypted only once both have answered; a keystore fault is still the error the caller gets, even when the object is also missing, and a failed key bills the tail read already sent.
