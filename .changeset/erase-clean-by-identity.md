An erasure opens again every generation it found clean before it answers `erased: true`, but the one it found current and its own rewrite, which it skips only while the row, read after its last listing of the bucket, names them with the footer checksum of the object it read or wrote; a load of the id that takes such a number during the erasure is then read, and the erasure answers `'superseded'` or throws `WriteConflictError`. A failed read of that row skips nothing. `eraseSubject`'s per-op budget charges a generation once, however often the call opens it.
