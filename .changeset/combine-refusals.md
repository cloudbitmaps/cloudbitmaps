An `*Into` whose destination is also one of its sources, and `intersect`, `union`, `intersectInto` and `unionInto` with an empty operand list, are refused with `ValidationError`. `PinnedAt` is a union of a numbered pin and a pin of no generation, so `pinAt` takes a pin's `pinnedAt` whole. The range, count and absent-operand errors name the verb that was asked.
