A refused segment ref or id says what is wrong with it and, for a load, where (`ids[1]: ...`), and a `null` or `undefined` ref throws a `ValidationError`, not a `TypeError`. A contention `WriteConflictError`, a rollback that loses its race and every `IntegrityError` the `.crbm` reader raises for bytes that fail its own checks end with what to do next (one from decrypting the index or a chunk keeps the cipher's message, since a re-load does not repair a wrong key), and an unsupported `.crbm` major version says which version the build reads. The `export-segments` command prints its settings for `--help` or `-h`, says what a missing `CR_EXPORT_ROOT` or `CR_EXPORT_OUT` is, and exits 2 for any argument but `--help`, `-h` or a leading `--`, so a script that passes one stops.
