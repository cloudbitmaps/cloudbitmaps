A refused segment ref or id says what is wrong with it and, for a load, where (`ids[1]: ...`), and a `null` or `undefined` ref throws a `ValidationError`, not a `TypeError`. A contention `WriteConflictError`, a rollback that loses its race and every `IntegrityError` the `.crbm` reader raises end with what to do next, and an unsupported `.crbm` major version says which version the build reads. The `export-segments` command prints its settings for `--help` or `-h`, says what a missing `CR_EXPORT_ROOT` or `CR_EXPORT_OUT` is, and exits 2 for any other argument, so a script that passes one stops.
